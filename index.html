Pocket GOAT
Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026.
App: Pocket GOAT
Developer: Kosmos Apps
Privacy and support contact: kosmosapps26@gmail.com

Pocket GOAT is an animated screen companion. It has no account signup, advertising, analytics, chat, or in-app purchases. The app has no Internet permission and does not send gameplay or device information to the developer or to third-party servers.

Optional home-screen icon finding

After your affirmative consent in Pocket GOAT and enabling the Android accessibility service, the app reads accessible icon labels, descriptions and screen positions on detected Android home screens. It also queries installed home-screen applications and launchable-app names to recognize icons. These details are used only to choose pretend goat bites and bird landing positions. They are processed locally in memory while the feature is enabled, including while the menu is closed. They are not written to files, uploaded, sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app does not click icons, open apps, inspect the contents inside other apps, take screenshots, record the screen, or remove apps. Android grants broader accessibility capabilities than this app uses.

Your controls and permissions

Icon finding is optional. You can decline it and still use the goat. Turn off icon finding in the app to withdraw in-app consent, or disable Pocket GOAT icon finder in Android Accessibility settings. Display-over-other-apps permission draws the goat, birds and chicken button over the screen. The ongoing notification lets you pause or stop the pet. Vibration is used for headbutts, and its strength is adjustable.

Motion and settings

Gravity, accelerometer and related motion-sensor readings make the animals react to turning or gentle shaking. Readings are used locally, are not recorded, and are not transmitted. Sound, tilt, vibration, landing-guide and consent preferences are stored in private Android app storage. Android backup for this app is disabled. The app does not access contacts, messages, photos, files outside its own resources, location, the camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or payment-card information.

Retention and deletion

Home-screen details and motion readings exist only in working memory and are cleared as the feature or service stops. Consent and settings stay on your device until changed, cleared through Android Settings > Apps > Pocket GOAT > Storage > Clear storage, or removed by uninstalling. Clearing storage resets your settings. No developer-hosted gameplay database or user account exists.

Children and families

Pocket GOAT is intended for a general audience including children. An adult should install the app and manage Android permissions. The app does not request children's names, ages, contact details, location or identifiers, and does not include advertising or social features. If a parent contacts support, the email provider delivers the information voluntarily included in that message. Please do not include children's personal information. Support correspondence is used to answer the request and is deleted when no longer needed for that purpose, unless retention is required by law.

Purchases and other services

Any purchase is handled by Google Play under its own privacy terms. Pocket GOAT does not receive your payment-card details. Google Play and Android may provide their own platform services, diagnostics or store statistics under their own policies; this app does not add a separate analytics or advertising service.

Security and updates

The app keeps preferences in its private Android storage and does not expose its services to ordinary third-party apps. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Material changes to optional icon access will require a renewed explanation and consent where applicable.
